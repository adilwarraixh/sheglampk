Product photo import

Drop in photos from your phone or camera. Each one is matched to a product, centre-cropped to portrait, resized to 1000×1200 and renamed to the exact filename the site expects. Then save them into assets/img/products/ and rebuild.

Shooting tip: a phone on a plain white surface next to a window beats a light box. Shoot portrait, fill about two-thirds of the frame with the product, and keep the same background for every shot so the grid looks consistent.
